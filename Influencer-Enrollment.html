FAQ

What founders ask before downloading

What exactly is the Meta Profit Funnel?

A complete, plug-and-play system that shows apparel brands how to run Meta ads profitably. You'll learn how top brands like Bewakoof, Souled Store and Suta structure their funnels — from awareness to conversion — with proven ad sets, creatives, and automation flows.

Is this a course or a PDF?

A comprehensive 44-page PDF playbook — not a long video course. You get actionable frameworks, templates, KPI benchmarks and examples that can be implemented immediately, even by small teams or solo founders.

Who is this for?
  • Apparel & fashion brand owners running ads but struggling with profit
  • Digital marketers & agencies managing fashion e-commerce clients
  • Early-stage D2C founders looking for a structured growth system
  • Anyone tired of random campaigns & ready for predictable, profitable funnels
Do I need to be a Meta Ads expert?

No. The playbook is written in a decision-based format — so even if you're new, you'll know what to do next. It covers setup, ad structures, creative briefs, tracking and optimisation in a clear, checklist-driven way.

How is this different from other ad guides or YouTube videos?

Because this isn't theory — it's built from real apparel brand data. You get the exact TOFU–MOFU–BOFU framework, KPI benchmarks, creative briefs per stage, profit math, and automation systems to recover lost sales. No fluff.

Will this work for my small or boutique brand?

Absolutely. The funnel system scales up or down based on your ad budget. Start lean, test small, and still get profitable results by following the Stop–Switch–Scale rules.

What results can I expect?

Within 48–72 hours you'll see directional signal — better CTR, lower CPLPV, higher ATCs. Within 2–4 weeks, most brands using this system stabilise at profitable CPA and ROAS. The more consistently you apply it, the more predictable your growth becomes.
