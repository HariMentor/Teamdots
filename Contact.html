Before you write

A few things people ask
before they send a brief

How fast do you reply?

Under 48 hours on weekdays, often the same day. Every brief gets a written response from a principal — not an account manager.

What size projects do you take?

One-off films from ₹2L, monthly retainers from ₹1.2L, full transformations from ₹5L+. We don't take on more than 8 retainers at a time — so the studio stays close to every founder.

Do you work outside India?

Yes — we work with founders in UAE, Singapore, UK and US. Production days happen anywhere; strategy and editing happen out of Bengaluru & Goa.

What if I'm just exploring?

Still write. We do free 30-min audits for genuine founders — no obligation. Some of our favourite engagements started as "just a chat."

Where can I see more work?

Our portfolio page covers brand films, social work and product launches. Instagram is the most-up-to-date feed.
