FAQ

The fine print, made simple

Can you really deliver in 14 days?

Yes — for the Business Revamp scope, when content and brand assets are ready on day 1. We only take 2 revamps per fortnight so we can hold the timeline. If you need a custom scope, we'll quote a different timeline upfront.

What platform do you build on?

Webflow and Framer for marketing sites; Shopify for ecommerce; Next.js for custom web apps. We pick what's easiest for you to maintain after handover, not what's easiest for us.

Do you do hosting and domain?

We set up hosting + DNS + emails + SSL as part of every build. You keep ownership of the accounts — no lock-in.

What if I don't have content or photos?

That's exactly what the add-ons are for. Bundle the Content Rewrite + Media at ₹15K and we'll write every word and shoot every image on the new site.

Is the SEO retainer monthly or contracted?

3-month minimum, then month-to-month. SEO doesn't move in 30 days; we'd rather under-promise than collect a fee that doesn't deliver.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 50% on launch. UPI, bank transfer, or Razorpay. International clients invoiced in USD/AED equivalent.
