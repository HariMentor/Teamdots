The problem

Marketing shouldn't require ten different platforms

Today's marketing stack is fragmented. Agencies managing multiple clients juggle hundreds of disconnected workflows. Small businesses spend valuable time learning tools instead of growing their business.

🧩

Campaign Fragmentation

Campaigns are planned in one tool, published in another, and measured somewhere else. Nothing connects.

⏰

Manual Optimization

Hours disappear into reporting, moving budgets, refreshing creatives, and updating campaigns.

📊

Disconnected Insights

Your social channels, ads, email, and website all generate valuable data — but none of it works together.

Meet OrbitIQ

Four AI agents, one continuous marketing engine

OrbitIQ replaces repetitive marketing work with autonomous AI agents that collaborate together. Instead of helping you create campaigns, OrbitIQ helps run them.

01 · Plan

Campaign Agent

Plans campaigns, allocates budgets, schedules content and coordinates every marketing channel from a single brief.

🗓️ Planning💰 Budget allocation📡 Channel coordination
02 · Create

Content Agent

Creates platform-specific copy, generates ad creatives, writes emails and maintains your brand voice across every touchpoint.

✍️ Copywriting🎨 Ad creatives📧 Email🎙️ Brand voice
03 · Measure

Analytics Agent

Tracks performance in real time, finds hidden opportunities, detects unusual changes and generates cross-channel insights.

📈 Real-time tracking🔎 Opportunity finding🚨 Anomaly detection

Optimization Agent

Adjusts campaign budgets, refreshes underperforming creatives, launches A/B tests and continuously improves performance.

🎯 Budget tuning🔄 Creative refresh🧪 A/B testing
