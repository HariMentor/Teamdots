FAQ

The questions founders actually ask us

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Most clients start with one — typically Visual Storytelling or Performance — and roll the others in once they see the engine working. The services are designed to stand alone and to compose.

What size brands do you work with?

Seed-stage startups through to Series-B founders, plus established consumer brands like Samyakk, Vilvah and Michelle Salins. We don't take on more than 8 retainers at a time — so we can stay genuinely close to every founder.

Where are you based? Do you travel?

Bangalore (#831, BTM 4th Stage), Trivandrum (B12, PPD) and Goa — with project travel across India. Production happens anywhere; strategy and editing are studio-side.

How is this different from MentorDots?

Teamdots is the next chapter of MentorDots. Same team, same craft — now built around founders and brands that need a studio for the AI era, not just a film unit.

What's the minimum engagement?

Three months for retainers, single-project minimums for film & photo. Strategy sprints are scoped at 4 weeks. We don't do month-to-month — it's never enough time to actually compound.

How fast can we start?

Typically 10–14 days from first call to kickoff. Send us a brief — we'll send back a point of view, three references, and a plan within 48 hours.
