Frequently asked

The honest questions founders ask

How early should I book my Onam campaign?

Ideally 6–8 weeks before Thiruvonam. The top creators are booked solid by mid-July — earlier you lock in, the better the network you'll get.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Star and Classic are fixed-scope, but Premium Custom is built around your brand, location, and goals — from 1 mega-creator to a 10-creator multi-week sprint with paid amplification.

Do I see the content before it goes live?

Always. Every piece of content runs through a two-round approval — internal QA at our studio, then your founder/marketing team. Nothing publishes without your green light.

What's your refund policy if a creator doesn't deliver?

If a contracted creator can't deliver for any reason, we replace them at no extra cost from our backup roster. If you choose to drop the campaign entirely, we refund the unspent portion within 7 days.

Do you work with brands outside Kerala?

Yes — Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai & Dubai brands have run Onam campaigns with us. The audience is Malayali; the brand can be from anywhere.

Will you handle paid amplification too?

Paid is included in the Premium Custom package (₹50K–₹1L). For other packages, we offer it as a per-month retainer add-on at ₹1.4L/mo + 10% of ad spend.
